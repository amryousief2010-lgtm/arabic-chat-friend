Privacy Policy (English summary)

Capital Ostrich Company (شركة نعام العاصمة), Egypt, sells ostrich meat products via https://coceg.net and its WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger and Instagram channels. We collect your name, phone number, delivery address, order details, the content of messages you send us and records of calls to our hotline, only to answer enquiries, process and deliver orders, provide customer service and improve quality. We never sell your data and share it only with delivery partners or when required by law. Data is stored on secured servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) under the company's own account, with access limited to authorised staff. Data received from Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp is used only to communicate with the customer who contacted us; it is never used for advertising or ad targeting and is never sold or shared. Customer messages and related data are kept for 24 months from the last interaction, then deleted or anonymised, except accounting records we are legally required to keep. You may request access, correction or deletion at any time by emailing capitalostrichs2010@gmail.com.
